## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

Dactor Clinic is the all-in-one outpatient (OPD) queue coordination, counter billing, and clinical management system built for clinics, polyclinics, diagnostic laboratories, and private hospitals across Nepal.

Transform your healthcare facility's daily operations. Eliminate reception bottlenecks, reduce patient wait times, broadcast live queue status directly to patient smartphones, and streamline reception counter workflows.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✨ CORE CLINIC FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📢 REAL-TIME OPD QUEUE BOARD & DOCTOR CALLING
• Intuitive token management for reception desks and doctor cabins.
• One-tap calling: Advance queue tokens (Waiting -> In Consultation -> Completed) with instant push alerts to patients.
• Live Doctor Presence indicator: Accurately indicate "In Cabin" or "Away" status.
• Public Queue Display mode: Connect to waiting room TV monitors or tablets for live token broadcasting.

🧾 RECEPTION COUNTER BILLING & INVOICE LEDGER
• Fast walk-in and online patient billing: Consultation fees, diagnostic lab tests, procedures, and medication.
• Multi-payment support: Record Cash at Desk, eSewa, Fonepay, or digital bank transfer.
• Print & Share official itemized fiscal invoices and consultation slips.
• Offline-Ready SQLite Ledger: Counter staff can continue registering patients and issuing receipts even during internet downtime; transactions automatically sync upon reconnection.

👨‍⚕️ DOCTOR SCHEDULING & ROSTER MANAGEMENT
• Manage multi-doctor clinical shifts, OPD session timings, and token quotas.
• Verified Doctor Profiles: Link NMC registration licenses, specialty qualifications, and room assignments.
• Future Appointments & Pay-At-Desk Request approvals with instant patient notification.

🧪 3-STAGE DIAGNOSTIC LABORATORY PIPELINE
• Manage lab orders seamlessly from collection to report delivery:
  1. Sample Collection Confirmation
  2. Testing & Processing
  3. PDF Report Upload & Certification
• Patients receive instant alerts and download certified PDF reports securely.

🔒 MULTI-DEVICE COUNTER PREFIX BINDING
• Multi-counter coordination: Assign independent device billing prefixes (e.g., Counter A, Counter B) to prevent invoice collision across simultaneous reception desks.

🛡️ CLINICAL CONFIDENTIALITY & GOVERNANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Dactor Clinic operates in strict accordance with Nepal healthcare standards:
• Nepal Individual Privacy Act 2075 & Privacy Rules 2077: Medical notes and patient diagnostic records are classified as sensitive personal data.
• Nepal Medical Council (NMC) Code of Ethics: Total doctor-patient confidentiality.
• End-to-End Encryption: TLS 1.3 transport encryption, multi-tenant Row-Level Security (RLS) isolation, and cryptographically signed URLs for private medical documents.
• Zero Data Selling: No patient or clinical data is ever sold or monetized.
