## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

Dactor is Nepal’s dedicated healthcare platform designed to eliminate waiting room crowding, provide real-time outpatient (OPD) queue visibility, and connect patients with verified doctors and diagnostic laboratories across Nepal.

Say goodbye to standing in crowded hospital corridors for hours. With Dactor, track your exact waiting token from home, your office, or a nearby cafe, and arrive right when the doctor is ready to see you.

✨ KEY PATIENT FEATURES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🏥 DISCOVER VERIFIED CLINICS & HOSPITALS
• Explore registered clinics, polyclinics, and diagnostic centers near you in Kathmandu, Janakpur, and across Nepal.
• Interactive OpenStreetMap and MapLibre routing with road travel estimates.
• View verified doctor profiles, specialty qualifications, NMC registration numbers, and transparent consultation fees.

⏱️ LIVE OPD QUEUE COUNTER & TRACKING
• Real-time queue broadcasting: See exactly which token number is currently inside the doctor’s consultation cabin.
• Dynamic countdown: Know how many patients are ahead of you and receive live estimated call times.
• Opt-in early call feature: Get notified if the clinic has an unexpected vacancy and you'd like to be called early.

📅 INSTANT DOCTOR APPOINTMENT BOOKING
• Book guaranteed appointment slots in seconds without waiting on hold.
• Automatic unique 10-digit booking tracking codes (e.g., DAC-9821-47) for counter check-in.
• Flexible payment: Choose between online pre-payment (eSewa / digital wallet) or "Pay at Desk" cash upon arrival.

🔬 3-STAGE CERTIFIED LAB REPORT DELIVERY
• Track diagnostic test processing in real-time:
  1. Sample Collected
  2. Testing in Laboratory
  3. Prepared & Certified
• Download official, cryptographically signed PDF laboratory reports directly on your mobile device.
• Secured with a Patient Identity Verification Gate to ensure strict medical confidentiality.

🔔 REAL-TIME NOTIFICATIONS & ALERTS
• Receive immediate push alerts when your token is next in line.
• Background active queue sync ensures you never miss a doctor consultation call.
